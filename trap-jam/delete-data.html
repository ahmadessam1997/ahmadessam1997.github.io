Delete your Trap Jam data

App: Trap Jam (com.ahmadessam.trapjam)
Last updated: 2 October 2026

Trap Jam has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is exactly what exists and how to remove each part.

1. Game progress — delete it yourself, instantly

Your stars, per-level best times, coin balance, death count and unlocked skins live only in the app's local storage on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

To erase it: uninstall Trap Jam, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Trap Jam → Storage → Clear data. This is immediate, permanent and needs no request from us.

2. Advertising identifier — reset it yourself

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID. We do not store it.

To reset or delete it: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, then "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".

You can also change your personalised-ads consent at any time from inside the game: open the Shop and use Ad privacy choices. That button stays available even after buying Remove Ads.

3. Gameplay analytics — cut the link, or request deletion

From version 1.4.0 Trap Jam sends anonymous gameplay events (for example "level cleared" or "shop opened") to Google Analytics for Firebase. They are held by Google and are tied to an app-instance ID and the advertising ID — never to a name, an email address or an account, because the game has none.

To cut the link yourself: delete the advertising ID (step 2) and clear the app's data or uninstall it (step 1). That discards the app-instance ID, so nothing collected afterwards is connected to the earlier events. Events already collected are deleted automatically by Google after the retention period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting).

To ask for earlier deletion, email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com with the subject "Trap Jam data deletion request" before you uninstall or clear the app's data. Because the events carry no name or email, we can only delete events that can be matched to your installation; we reply within 30 days saying what was deleted.

4. Leaderboard scores — only exist if you signed in

Trap Jam has 26 Google Play Games leaderboards: one per level, plus Levels Completed and Total Speedrun Time. If you never signed in to Play Games, nothing was ever posted and there is nothing to delete.

If you did sign in, your submitted times are held by Google Play Games under your Play Games player ID, not by us.

To delete them: open the Play Games app → profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, where you can remove your data for this game individually or for all games. We cannot delete a Play Games score on your behalf.

5. Purchase records — request deletion

If you bought Remove Ads or a skin, a record of that purchase is held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, tied to an anonymous random ID and to your Google Play account.

To request deletion of the RevenueCat record, email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com with the subject "Trap Jam data deletion request" and include:

  1. The Google Play order number of any purchase (it is in your Play Store purchase-history email, and looks like GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567), or
  2. The email address of the Google Play account used for the purchase.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, and you will get a confirmation email when it is done.

Please read before requesting: deleting a purchase record means the purchase can no longer be restored on a new device or after a reinstall, and it is not a refund. Refunds are handled separately by Google Play.

What is kept, and for how long

  • We run no servers and keep no database of players.
  • On-device data (stars, times, coins, skins): kept until you uninstall or clear the app's data.
  • Leaderboard scores: kept by Google Play Games until you delete them there.
  • Purchase records: kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as the purchase is restorable, or until you request deletion as above.
  • Gameplay analytics: kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for 2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting, then deleted automatically.
  • Advertising data: retained under Google's advertising policy.
