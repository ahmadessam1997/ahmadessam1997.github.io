Trap Jam — Privacy Policy

App: Trap Jam (com.ahmadessam.trapjam)
Effective date: 2 October 2026 (applies from version 1.4.0)
Contact: ahmadessam1997@gmail.com

The short version

Trap Jam has no account and no login. Your stars, best times, coins, deaths, daily streak and unlocked skins are stored on your device only.

The game does four things that involve other companies: it shows ads (Google AdMob), it sells optional one-time purchases (Google Play Billing, handled through RevenueCat), it measures how the game is played with anonymous gameplay events (Google Analytics for Firebase), and — only if you choose to sign in — it posts your clear times to Google Play Games leaderboards. It can also show a daily reminder notification, which is created on your device and involves no other company. Everything those services receive is described below, and it matches the Data safety answers on the Google Play listing exactly.

The game never asks for and never receives your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera. It has no text input at all. Payment card details are entered into Google Play, never into Trap Jam — we never see them.

Advertising, and the AD_ID permission

Trap Jam is free and is paid for by ads. The app declares the Android permission com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, which the Google Mobile Ads SDK needs in order to read your device's advertising ID on Android 13 and later. That permission is the reason this policy — and the Play data-safety form — can no longer say "no data collected".

Six ad formats appear, and only in these places:

  • Banner — on the menu and the level-clear card only. It is hidden the instant a level loads, so it never sits under your jumps.
  • Native ad — a small ad card on the level-clear card. When it shows, the banner does not.
  • Interstitial — a full-screen ad in the seam between levels, after you dismiss the clear card. Never while you are playing, and never when you die.
  • Rewarded interstitial — the same between-levels seam, occasionally, and it pays coins.
  • App open ad — when you come back to the game after at least 30 seconds away, and only on the menu or the level-clear card. Never when the game is first opened and never over a level in progress.
  • Reward video — opt-in only. You can choose to watch one in the shop for coins, on the level-clear card to double the coins of that clear, or from the pause card to skip a level you are stuck on.

Ad requests from this game are capped to the "General" content rating, and the game does not request child-directed or under-age-of-consent treatment.

Consent, and how to change it later

If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region where consent is required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows you a consent form before the ads SDK is initialized, so no ad request is made under an unknown consent state. If consent is required and you have not granted it, ads are requested non-personalised rather than personalised.

You can change that decision at any time: open the Shop in the game and tap "Ad privacy choices" in the footer. That button reopens Google's privacy options form and is shown whenever Google reports that a privacy-options entry point is required — including for players who have bought Remove Ads, because owning the purchase does not remove your right to revise a consent record.

Reward videos are optional

A reward video is never forced on you. There are three offers, and each is a button you choose to tap: coins in the shop, "double coins" on the level-clear card, and "skip this level", which appears on the pause card only after many deaths on the same level. Declining costs you nothing but the reward. The coin skins, the trails and the skip tokens are bought with coins earned simply by playing, so you never have to watch an ad.

Analytics

Trap Jam uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record the anonymous gameplay events listed in the table above. They tell us things like "most players die 30 times on level 13" or "nobody opens the shop", so levels and pacing can be fixed. An event never contains your name, an account, or anything you typed — the game has no text input. Google attaches its own app-instance ID, your device's advertising ID, device model, OS version, country and language.

You can limit this: resetting or deleting your advertising ID (see Your choices below) unlinks future events from the old ID, and where consent is required your answer on the consent form governs how Google may use this data for advertising. Google keeps analytics events for a limited period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting) and then deletes them automatically.

Notifications

Trap Jam can show one reminder notification that a new daily level is ready, about a day after you last played. It is scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to a server and there is no push-messaging token.

The game asks for permission to show notifications only after you finish a daily level for the first time — never when it is first opened. If you say no, nothing is scheduled and you are not asked again. You can turn the reminder off at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Trap Jam → Notifications.

Removing ads

The one-time Remove Ads purchase permanently stops every ad format — banner, native, interstitial, rewarded interstitial, app open and the opt-in reward video. The reward-video offers (coins, double coins, skip a level) are hidden rather than merely refused, the level skip is offered for coins instead, and the app stops asking the ads SDK to load anything at all. Anonymous analytics events are still recorded. The purchase is restored on any device signed in to the same Google Play account; a Restore Purchases button in the shop re-checks it.

Leaderboards are optional

Trap Jam is fully playable signed out. Nothing is locked behind Play Games, and declining sign-in costs you no content, no stars and no skins.

If you do sign in to Google Play Games, the game submits your per-level clear times, your number of levels completed, and — once every level has a recorded time — your total speedrun time, to Google's leaderboards. Those scores and your Play Games profile are then visible to other players in the rankings, exactly as Play Games leaderboards work everywhere. Nothing else is sent to Play Games: not your deaths, not your coins, not your purchases, not your skins. A level you skipped posts no time and does not count as completed there, and the daily level has no leaderboard.

Your Play Games profile, its visibility and your leaderboard entries are managed by Google, in the Play Games app under Settings, not by us.

What stays on your device

Levels cleared or skipped, stars (up to 5 per level, 200 total), best time per level, your coin balance, your lifetime death count, which skins and trails you own and which you have equipped, your skip tokens, your daily-level streak, how many levels remain before the next rating prompt or between-levels ad, and whether you have been asked about notifications. These are records in the app's own storage. It is not transmitted anywhere, there is no cloud save, and uninstalling Trap Jam deletes it permanently.

Children

Trap Jam is rated for everyone, but because it uses the advertising ID it is not directed at children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before playing. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you want any associated advertising identifier removed, contact us at the address above.

Your choices, and how to delete data

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads: Android Settings → Google → All services → Ads. You can delete the advertising ID entirely there; ads will still appear, but will not be personalised.
  • Change your ad consent: Shop → Ad privacy choices, as described above.
  • Turn off the daily reminder: Android Settings → Apps → Trap Jam → Notifications.
  • Analytics data: it is keyed to the advertising ID and an app-instance ID. Deleting the advertising ID and uninstalling the game (or clearing its data) cuts the link; deletion requests are explained on our data deletion page.
  • Delete everything stored on the device: uninstalling Trap Jam erases your stars, times, coins, deaths and unlocked skins permanently. There is no cloud copy for us to keep.
  • Leaderboard entries: remove them through the Google Play Games app, or by deleting your Play Games profile for this game — Google holds those scores, not us.
  • Purchase records: these are held by Google Play and RevenueCat as proof of purchase. Request deletion at our data deletion page, or by emailing the address above. Note that deleting a purchase record means the associated unlock can no longer be restored.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play under its own policy.

Data retention

We operate no servers and keep no database of our own. Retention is governed by the processors: Google's advertising data retention policy for ad data, Google Analytics for Firebase's retention setting for gameplay events (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting), RevenueCat's for purchase records, and Google Play Games' for leaderboard scores. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data immediately.
