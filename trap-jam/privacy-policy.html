Trap Jam — Privacy Policy

App: Trap Jam (com.ahmadessam.trapjam)
Effective date: 24 September 2026
Contact: ahmadessam1997@gmail.com

The short version

Trap Jam has no account and no login. Your stars, best times, coins, deaths and unlocked skins are stored on your device only and are never uploaded.

The game does three things that involve other companies: it shows ads (Google AdMob), it sells optional one-time purchases (Google Play Billing, handled through RevenueCat), and — only if you choose to sign in — it posts your clear times to Google Play Games leaderboards.

What is collected, and by whom

We operate no servers and hold no database of players. Everything below is collected by Google or RevenueCat under their own policies.

Advertising, and the AD_ID permission

Trap Jam declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission. The Google Mobile Ads SDK needs it on Android 13 and above to read the device advertising ID. That single permission is the reason this app can no longer claim "no data collected" on its Play data-safety form — and the reason this policy exists in its current form.

Ads appear in four places and nowhere else:

  • a banner on the menu and the level-clear card — never during play, because a strip along the bottom of the screen sits exactly where you judge a jump from;
  • an interstitial in the gap between levels — never when you die;
  • a rewarded video, which is entirely opt-in and pays coins;
  • a rewarded interstitial between levels, which also pays coins.

Consent, and how to change it later

In the EEA, the UK and the US states that require it, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your consent choices before any ad loads. You can change that choice at any time: open the Shop and tap Ad privacy choices.

That button stays available even after you buy Remove Ads. The consent record exists whether or not ads are being served to you, and you are entitled to revise it either way.

Reward videos are optional

You never have to watch one. Every skin in the game can also be bought with coins earned simply by playing.

Removing ads

The one-time Remove Ads purchase suppresses every ad format, including the opt-in rewarded videos, and stops the app requesting ads at all.

Leaderboards are optional

Trap Jam has 26 Google Play Games leaderboards: one per level ranking clear times, plus Levels Completed and Total Speedrun Time. Sign-in is optional and the game is fully playable signed out — every leaderboard failure is silent by design. If you do not sign in, nothing is ever transmitted to Play Games.

What stays on your device

Stars, per-level best times, the coin balance, the death counter, which skins you own and which is equipped. This is kept in the app's private storage and is removed when you uninstall or clear the app's data.

Children

Trap Jam is not directed at children under 13 and is not enrolled in Google Play's Designed for Families programme. It serves standard advertising and is rated for a general teen-and-up audience.

Your choices, and how to delete data

Full instructions, including resetting your advertising ID, deleting Play Games scores and requesting deletion of a purchase record, are on the Delete your data page.
