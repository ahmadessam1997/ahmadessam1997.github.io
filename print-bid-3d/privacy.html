3D Print Bid: Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

3D Print Bid is operated by Ahmed Afifi ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who receives it. The app has no advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising id, or usage analytics. It asks for photo or file access only when you choose a file or photo to upload.

Service providers

  • Stripe (payments, payouts, identity verification): stripe.com/privacy
  • Shippo (shipping rates, labels, tracking), when enabled: goshippo.com/privacy
  • Fly.io (server hosting)

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Model files and photos of requests that were cancelled or expired: deleted with your account.
  • Order, payment, dispute and rating records: kept for up to 7 years after the order, detached from your identity when you delete your account, because tax and fraud-prevention rules require records of transactions.
  • If an account was banned, a one-way hash of its email is kept so the ban cannot be evaded.

Your choices

  • Edit your name and addresses in the app.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile > Delete account, or see account deletion.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by email. Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EEA/UK) you may have further rights to access, delete or object; we honour such requests from any user.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed. Access to files is checked on every download. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your account.

Children

The app is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We will post changes here and, for significant changes, show a notice in the app.
