3D Print Bid: Terms and Marketplace Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

3D Print Bid is a marketplace app. Buyers post a 3D model they want printed, makers (sellers) bid to print and ship it, and the buyer picks a maker by price and rating. 3D Print Bid is operated by Ahmed Afifi ("we"). By creating an account you agree to these terms.

The rule behind every section below: whoever causes a cost pays it. We are the venue, not the maker, the buyer or the carrier. We do not make, inspect, store or ship prints.

1. Accounts

  • You must be 18 or older and give true information. One account per person.
  • The service is open to buyers and makers worldwide. All prices, payments and payouts are in US dollars; your card or bank converts them. Makers are paid through Stripe, so a maker must live in a country where Stripe can pay out.
  • Makers must complete payout verification with our payment processor (Stripe) and keep a valid card on file (section 8) before they can bid.
  • You are responsible for what happens under your account.

2. Posting a request (buyers)

  • Upload your model (3MF preferred because it carries colours and print settings; STL, STEP and OBJ are also accepted), describe material, colour and quantity, and set the price range you are willing to pay for the print.
  • Your price range is for the print only. Each maker offers one or more shipping options (for example economy and express); you choose one when you accept a bid and pay its price.
  • You confirm that you own the model or have the right to have it printed. See section 11.
  • Makers bidding on your request see your city, region, postal code and country only. Your full address is shown only to the maker you pay. Your model file can be downloaded only by the maker you pay, unless you switch on "let bidders download".
  • Requests stay open for 14 days.

3. Bids (makers)

  • A bid states the print price, one to five shipping options with their prices, and the number of days you need before shipping. A bid is a binding offer while it is active. You can change or withdraw it until the buyer accepts it.
  • By bidding you confirm you can print the model as described (size, material, colour, quantity).

5. Payment is held until the order is accepted

  • After accepting a bid the buyer has 24 hours to pay; otherwise the order is cancelled and the request reopens.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. The payment is held and not paid to the maker while the order is being printed, shipped and inspected.
  • The maker is paid when the buyer accepts the order, or automatically when the inspection period (section 7) ends without a problem being reported.
  • Paying or arranging payment outside the app is not allowed and removes all protection in this policy.

6. Printing and shipping

  • The maker must ship within the number of days in the bid, plus 2 days' grace, using a tracked service, and enter the carrier and tracking number in the app.
  • If the maker has not shipped by that date, the buyer can cancel for a full refund. One week later the order is refunded automatically.
  • The maker is responsible for the parcel until it is delivered: packaging, loss and damage in transit are the maker's risk. Makers should insure valuable parcels and claim from the carrier.
  • An order counts as delivered when the carrier reports delivery, when the buyer confirms receipt, or 14 days after shipping (30 days when maker and buyer are in different countries) if neither happens and no not-received report is open.
  • Shipping labels. In the bid the maker states the packed parcel (box size and weight). The buyer then sees live carrier rates from the maker's area to the buyer's address, chooses one and pays for it with the order. The label is bought from that payment when the buyer pays, and appears in the order for the maker to print. If the parcel the maker hands over is bigger or heavier than declared, any carrier surcharge is the maker's. If the order is cancelled before shipping, the unused label is voided. Makers may instead offer their own shipping options, buy their own label and enter the carrier and tracking number (a photo of the label or receipt can be attached).
  • International orders. The maker ships with the option the buyer chose, declares the parcel truthfully for customs at the print price, and uses a tracked service. Import duties, taxes and customs fees in the buyer's country are the buyer's to pay; a parcel refused or abandoned because these were not paid counts as delivered. Items that cannot be legally sent to the buyer's country may not be ordered.

7. Inspection: 3 days to accept or report a problem

  • After delivery the buyer has 3 days to accept the order or report a problem in the app.
  • If the buyer does neither, the order is accepted automatically after 3 days and the maker is paid. This also applies if a buyer is unhappy but does not report it in time, or reports it and then does not send the item back. An accepted order is final.
  • Valid problems: not received, damaged, defective print, not as described (wrong size, material, colour or quantity) or wrong item. Photos are required.
  • Prints are made to order. A change of mind is not a valid problem; a return for that reason is possible only if the maker agrees.

8. Problems, returns and refunds

When a buyer reports a problem the money stays on hold. The maker has 3 days to choose one of:

  1. Refund in full without a return.
  2. Ask for the item back. For a faulty, damaged or wrong print the maker must supply a prepaid return label. The buyer must ship within 5 days and enter the tracking number. The refund is released when the maker confirms the return, or automatically 3 days after the return is delivered if the maker reports nothing.
  3. Offer a partial refund and let the buyer keep the print. If the buyer declines, we review the case.
  4. Disagree, with an explanation and evidence. We then review the evidence from both sides and decide.

If the maker does not respond within 3 days to a fault report, the buyer is refunded in full.

Wrong or empty returns

If what comes back is not the item that was sent, the maker must report it within 3 days of the return being delivered, with at least two photos: the parcel showing its shipping label, and its contents. We review the proof. If it is upheld the buyer receives no refund and is banned. Makers who file false return reports are banned.

Costs charged to makers

Makers authorise us to charge the card on file for costs they cause under the table above. If the card is declined, the amount is owed: it is deducted from the next payout and the maker cannot bid until it is settled.

Card chargebacks

Use the in-app process first. If a buyer files a chargeback with their bank instead, we submit the order record (tracking, messages, photos, acceptance) as evidence. A chargeback filed for an order that was accepted, or to avoid this process, leads to a ban. A chargeback decided against the party at fault is charged to them.

9. Ratings

  • After an order ends, buyer and maker can rate each other from 1 to 5 stars with a comment. Ratings are public and help buyers choose between bids.
  • Ratings must describe the real order. We remove ratings that are abusive, reveal personal data, or are used to demand money or a refund.

10. Strikes and bans

  • A maker gets a strike for cancelling a paid order, not shipping in time, ignoring a fault report, or losing a reviewed dispute. Makers who cooperate (refund or accept the return) get no strike. Three strikes suspend the account.
  • We ban accounts for fraud, false claims, wrong or empty returns, taking payment outside the app, harassment, prohibited items, or evading a previous ban.
  • Orders already paid continue to their normal end after a ban.

11. What may not be requested or printed

  • Firearms, firearm parts, suppressors, magazines, or parts that make a weapon automatic; other weapons where unlawful.
  • Models you have no right to reproduce: copyrighted or trademarked designs without the owner's permission or a licence that allows it (many free model licences forbid commercial printing).
  • Keys you are not entitled to copy, lock-bypass tools, skimmer parts, counterfeit goods, drug paraphernalia.
  • Sexually explicit, hateful or harassing content; anything illegal where the buyer or maker lives.
  • Items sold as safe for food, medical, child-safety or load-bearing safety use. Desktop 3D prints are not certified for these uses.

Report a request, user, message or rating with the Report button. We review reports and remove content and accounts that break these rules. Rights owners can send a notice to the contact address below with the listing, proof of ownership and a signed statement; we remove infringing requests and close repeat infringers' accounts.

12. Messages and conduct

Order chat is for the order. No harassment, spam, or requests to pay or talk outside the app. You can block any user; blocked users cannot bid on your requests and you do not see theirs. We may read order messages when reviewing a dispute or a report.

13. Taxes

Makers are responsible for their own income and sales taxes. Stripe may issue tax forms to makers and collect tax identification details as required by law.

14. Our role and liability

  • The sale contract is between buyer and maker. We provide the app, hold and release the payment according to this policy, and decide disputes that the two sides cannot settle. Our dispute decisions are final within the app.
  • We do not guarantee that a print fits a purpose, is safe, or that a maker or buyer will perform. The app is provided "as is".
  • To the extent the law allows, our total liability for an order is limited to the fees we received for that order, and we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss.

15. Ending your account

You can delete your account in the app (Profile > Delete account) once you have no orders in progress and owe nothing. See how deletion works.

16. Changes

We may update this policy. For changes that affect money or disputes we show a notice in the app before they take effect. Orders follow the policy in force when they were paid.
