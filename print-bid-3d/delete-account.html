3D Print Bid: Delete your account

App by Ahmed Afifi

In the app

  1. Open 3D Print Bid and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and confirm.

Deletion is immediate. It is possible once you have no orders in progress and owe nothing.

Without the app

Email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject "Delete my 3D Print Bid account". We delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, password, addresses and maker profile.
  • Your open requests and bids, notifications and block list.
  • Model files and photos of requests that were cancelled or expired.

What is kept

  • Records of paid orders (amounts, tracking, messages, problem reports, ratings) for up to 7 years, shown as "Deleted user" and no longer linked to your identity. Tax and fraud-prevention rules require this.
  • Payment records held by Stripe under its own policy.
  • For banned accounts only: a one-way hash of the email, so the ban cannot be evaded.
