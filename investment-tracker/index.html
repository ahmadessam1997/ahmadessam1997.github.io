Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app opens straight into your portfolio with a 7-day free trial. An account (email, Google or Apple) is only asked for when you subscribe or restore a subscription, so the subscription follows you to another device.

Where is my portfolio stored?

On your device only. It is never uploaded to us. Use More → Export CSV to keep a backup before changing phones.

How do I import my broker statement?

More → Import, then pick a CSV or PDF statement from your files. Supported formats are listed on the import screen; if yours is missing, email us a sample with the figures changed.

My subscription is not recognised on a new device

Sign in with the same account you subscribed with, then open More → Subscription → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel?

  • Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Investment Tracker → Cancel.
  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Investment Tracker → Cancel.

You keep access until the end of the paid period.

Price alerts did not fire

Alerts are checked in the background about every 15 minutes on Android; on iPhone, iOS decides when background refresh runs (usually while charging or on Wi-Fi). Make sure notifications are allowed for the app and that Low Power Mode / battery saver is off.

How do I delete my account?

More → Delete Account inside the app, or see Delete your account.
