Privacy Policy — Investment Tracker

Investment Tracker ("the App", "we", "us" or "our"), operated by Ahmad Essam ("the Developer"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices and rights you have. By using the App, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the App.

  • App: Investment Tracker (Android: com.ahmadessam.investmenttracker on Google Play; iPhone: the same bundle identifier on the App Store)

2. The short version

  • You can use the App without an account. It opens straight into your portfolio with a 7-day free trial that starts the first time you open it.
  • An account (email, Google or Apple) is only asked for when you subscribe or restore a subscription, so the subscription can follow you to another device.
  • Your portfolio data stays on your device. It is not uploaded to us, with or without an account.
  • The App shows no advertising, does not use the advertising ID and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • The App records anonymous usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase) to help us improve it.
  • The App can send local notifications for price alerts you set, and checks prices periodically in the background to do so.
  • You can delete your account inside the App (More → Delete Account) at any time.

3. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to provide the App's features.

3.1 Information you provide

  • Portfolio data you enter: investment transactions, holdings, watchlists, price alerts, goals and notes you add or import (for example from a CSV or PDF broker statement). This is stored on your device only.
  • Account information (only if you create or sign in to an account): your email address and password (email sign-up); your name, email address and profile picture as provided by Google (Google sign-in); or your name and email address — or the private relay address Apple generates if you choose "Hide My Email" — as provided by Apple (Sign in with Apple). When you sign up with email we send a verification email; verifying is recommended but not required to use the App.

Earlier versions of the App asked for a billing address at registration. The current version does not. If you registered with an earlier version, that address may still be stored with your account until you delete the account (see section 8).

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Trial start date: the date you first opened the App is stored on your device to run the 7-day trial. If you create an account, a trial start date is also stored with the account.
  • Subscription status: whether you are on a trial, subscribed or expired, and your plan and renewal status. Purchases are tied to an anonymous purchase ID until you sign in, and to your account ID afterwards.
  • Authentication data (accounts only): technical tokens used to keep you signed in, and your email-verification status.
  • Usage analytics: the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record app events. These are: sign-up started/completed, demo portfolio loaded, a transaction logged (its type and sector only — never the ticker, amount or price), a broker import completed (broker format and number of rows), the subscription screen shown, purchase started/completed (plan identifier), purchases restored, a price alert created or triggered (direction only), live prices turned on, and responses to the in-app rating prompt. Google Analytics also collects standard technical information automatically: an app-instance identifier, device model, OS version, app version, language, country/region (derived from the IP address, which is not stored) and session/engagement data. The advertising ID (Android) and the IDFA (iOS) are not collected; on iOS the App never asks for tracking permission because it does not track.

3.3 Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not upload your portfolio (transactions, holdings, watchlist, alerts, goals) to our servers.
  • We do not collect or store your payment card numbers. All subscription payments are processed by Google Play Billing (Android) or the App Store (iPhone).
  • We do not show ads, do not use the advertising ID or IDFA, and do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. How we use your information

  • Provide the App's core features (portfolio tracking, analytics, imports, alerts);
  • Run your free trial and manage your subscription, including renewals, cancellations and restoring a subscription on another device;
  • Create and secure your account, if you choose to create one;
  • Fetch market prices for the symbols in your portfolio, watchlist and price alerts;
  • Notify you when a price alert you set is reached;
  • Understand, in aggregate, how the App is used so we can improve it;
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important account or service notices;
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse or technical problems;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. Where your data is stored

  • On your device: your portfolio entries, holdings, watchlists, alerts, goals, settings and the trial start date. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes them.
  • In the cloud (accounts only): your account record (email, trial start date and subscription status) is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access is restricted so that each user can only read and write their own record.
  • Analytics and subscription records are held by Google (Firebase Analytics) and RevenueCat respectively.

7. Market data, price alerts, notifications and background activity

  • To show prices, the App sends the ticker symbols in your portfolio, watchlist and price alerts to a market-data provider. These requests contain the symbols only — not your identity, account or quantities. As with any internet request, the provider sees your IP address.
  • If you set a price alert, the App checks prices for the alert's symbols periodically in the background, including when the App is closed (roughly every 15 minutes at best on Android; on iPhone, iOS decides when background refresh runs, typically while the device is charging or on Wi-Fi). Background checks only run while you have at least one pending alert and stop when your access ends.
  • When a target is reached the App shows a local notification created on your device. No push service or server is involved and no notification token is collected. Notification permission is requested when you create your first alert, never at launch; you can turn notifications off in your device settings at any time and alerts will still appear inside the App.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Device data: use More → Reset All Data in the App to clear your portfolio data, or uninstall the App.
  • Account data: we keep your account record for as long as your account exists. To delete your account and its cloud record immediately, open the App and use More → Delete Account. You can also email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com from the account's email address and we will delete it within 30 days, except where retention is required by law. See Delete your account.
  • Analytics data is not linked to your name or email and is retained by Google Analytics for a limited period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting), then deleted automatically. Clearing the App's data or reinstalling resets the app-instance identifier.
  • Subscription/payment records held by Google Play, Apple and RevenueCat are subject to their retention policies.
