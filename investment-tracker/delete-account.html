Delete your Investment Tracker account and data

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your Investment Tracker account and the data associated with it. You can use the app without an account; an account exists only if you created one to subscribe or to restore a subscription.

Option 1 — Delete the data stored on your device

Open the app and go to More → Reset All Data. This permanently clears all of your portfolio data (transactions, holdings, watchlist, alerts, goals and settings) stored on your device. Your portfolio is stored on your device only and is never uploaded to us.

Option 2 — Delete your account in the app

Signed in? Open the app and go to More → Delete Account, then confirm. You may be asked to confirm with Apple or Google first. This deletes your account, its sign-in and its cloud record (trial start date and subscription status) straight away.

Option 3 — Delete your account by email

To delete your account and every piece of data linked to it, email ahmadessam1997@gmail.com from the email address used for your account, with the subject line "Delete my account." We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email, Google or Apple sign-in identity)
  • Your name (if provided via Google or Apple sign-in)
  • Your billing address — older accounts only. The current version of the app does not ask for or collect a billing address. Earlier versions asked for one at registration; if you registered with one of those versions, the stored address is deleted together with your account.
  • Your account's cloud record (trial start date and subscription status)
  • Your subscription records held by our payment provider (RevenueCat)

Usage analytics

The app's anonymous usage events (Google Analytics for Firebase) are not linked to your name, email or account, so they cannot be looked up from an account. They are tied to an app-instance identifier that is reset when you clear the app's data or reinstall, and Google deletes them automatically after the retention period (2 to 14 months depending on our Google Analytics setting).

What may be retained

We may retain a minimal set of records where required to comply with legal, tax, accounting or fraud-prevention obligations. Such records are kept only for as long as the law requires and are then deleted.

Note about your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription.

  • Android: open Google Play → Subscriptions (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions) and cancel Investment Tracker there.
  • iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or the App Store → your account → Subscriptions) and cancel Investment Tracker there.
